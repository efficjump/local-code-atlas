# Code of conduct

Local Code Atlas contributors are expected to keep participation respectful,
constructive, and focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private source or
credentials, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Review code
and claims rather than attacking people. Treat reports of false positives and
false negatives as opportunities to improve reproducibility.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms contributors or users. Sensitive conduct concerns should be
reported through the repository's private maintainer contact rather than a
public issue.
